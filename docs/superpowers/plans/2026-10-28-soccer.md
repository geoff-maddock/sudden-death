# Soccer, the tenth sport: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add soccer as Sudden Death's tenth card-game sport, at the level of the under-served sports (golf, bowling, jousting, curling, boxing), and make the Rimewatch Sheet the Realm's soccer ground, so every ground has its own sport.

**Architecture:** Soccer is one more key in the existing per-sport tables and `switch (B.sport)` cases. No new systems. The Realm's ground `g_sheet` keeps its id (it is in saves: home grounds, records, fixtures) and changes sport.

**Tech Stack:** Plain JS in one global scope (`src/manifest.txt`, `src/realm/manifest.txt`), Canvas 2D, Python Playwright checks.

**Spec:** GitHub issue #10 ("Soccer: a tenth sport, to the same level as the other nine").

## Decisions (Geoff's answers on #10; the values marked "my call" are defaults to change)

1. **The rule, "Pass and move" (Geoff: good):**
   - Every card you play is a pass.
   - An Attack played with 3+ passes is a shot. It hits +1 per pass, at most +6 (my call), shown as "Passes" in the damage breakdown.
   - A shot that puts 10+ on one enemy is a goal: 1 point, and you draw a card. Scored or not, the passes reset to 0.
   - Passes reset at the start of each of your turns.
   - An enemy hit that gets 5+ through your Block is a goal for them.
   - **Geoff:** "if you block all damage in a round, that's a goal prevented." So an enemy turn where they hit you and nothing gets through is a **save**. It shows a "Goal prevented" banner, counts in `B.sc.saves`, and starts your next turn with 1 pass, the keeper's throw to start the counter (my call). Three saves is a highlight, "Brick wall".
2. **The ground (Geoff: good name):** the Hoarfrost Pitch, a snow pitch. The abbey still keeps it, so Sister Hogline stays as its keeper.
3. **The squad (Geoff: 11 total, a minimum of 5):** eleven a side. Five of your own players at least, and local call-ups fill the rest, as baseball's nine does. `W_GRD_SQUAD.soccer = 5`, with `W_GRD_WHY.soccer = 'eleven a side: five of your own, and call-ups fill the rest'`.
4. **Balance (Geoff: same as the other major sports):** soccer is a major sport like baseball, hockey, football and basketball.
   - **Schedule:** `ACT_SPORTS` weights 2, 2 and 3, like basketball.
   - **A constellation:** every major sport has one, so soccer gets "The Net", with soccer cards hitting 1 harder.
   - **Balance target:** its sports-win rate sits in the major sports' range in `lab.py`.

## Global constraints

- **Words:** user-facing text is plain, short and specific (CLAUDE.md).
- **Explained damage:** the pass bonus goes through `atkDmg`'s `add()`, labelled `Passes`.
- **Plain data:** `B` stays plain JSON, so `B.sc.passes` is a number.
- **Realm names:** realm top-level names start with `w`/`W`. Wrap by reassignment only.
- **Builds:** rebuild and commit both `dist/` files at every commit that changes `src/`.
- **Tests:** every new check asserts and exits nonzero on failure. Each goes in `test/all.sh` and the CI `CHECKS` list.

## Review focus

- **Passes across turns:** they reset at the start of your turn, never carry over (`sportTurnBegin`).
- **Damage that isn't an Attack:** a Play or Skill that deals damage is a pass, not a shot.
- **Stopper perk:** it raises the goal line by 3, as for hockey.
- **Old saves at the Rimewatch Sheet:** a home ground there stays yours. Its curling record is cleared once, with a log line. A fixture already drawn there plays as soccer.
- **The baron:** his quarter-sport list (`QUARTER_SPORT`) is unchanged, so soccer never appears there by accident.
